Key Findings

Most influential feature: Scheduled Departure Time (CRSDepTime).

Second most influential: Destination Airport.

Third most influential: Origin Airport.

What does this mean?

The model relies most heavily on scheduled departure time when distinguishing delayed and non-delayed flights in the validation dataset. Origin and destination airports also contribute to its predictions. Year and Month showed zero measured permutation importance in this evaluation.

These results describe model behavior. They do not establish that a feature causes a flight delay. They are global explanations, not explanations of an individual flight prediction.
